# ByteVerse 2026 — Official Participant Handbook & Rules

**Welcome to ByteVerse 2026!**  
Organized by NSDC at Vidyavardhini's College of Engineering and Technology (VCET), Vasai.

---

## 1. Tournament Format & Structure

ByteVerse is a **duo-based technical coding competition**. You and your teammate will work together as a unit across 5 distinct competitive stages:

| Round | Challenge Name | Duration | Format |
|:---:|---|:---:|---|
| **Round 1** | **Logical Thinking** | 20 Minutes | Logic Tracing & Output Prediction (MCQ) |
| **Round 2** | **AI Code Optimization** | 25 Minutes | Refactoring & Debugging Inefficient Code |
| **Round 3** | **Debugging & Code Analysis** | 35 Minutes | Algorithmic Problem Solving |
| **Round 4** | **Data Structures & Algorithms** | 45 Minutes | Advanced DSA & Type Transformations |
| **Round 5** | **AI vs Human (Grand Finale)** | 35 Minutes | High-Velocity Competitive Coding |

---

## 2. Duo Team Collaboration & Problem Sets

- **Team Composition:** Exactly two registered participants (1 Captain / Leader and 1 Team Member).
- **Set A vs. Set B Distribution:**
  - The **Team Leader** will automatically be assigned **Set A** problems.
  - The **Team Member** will automatically be assigned **Set B** problems.
- **Team Score Calculation:** Your team's total score is the **average** of both teammates' scores. Both members must perform to achieve a top leaderboard standing!

---

## 3. Integrated AI Assistant & Rules of Use

ByteVerse features a built-in AI Assistant to guide you when stuck:
- **Concept Mode (EXPLAIN):** Provides Socratic hints and explains underlying logic without giving away answers. **Using this caps your maximum score for that round at 75%.**
- **Syntax Mode (CODE):** Assists with language-specific syntax and boilerplate patterns. **Using this caps your maximum score for that round at 50%.**
- **Tournament AI Limits:** You have a lifetime quota of **15 EXPLAIN** prompts and **25 CODE** prompts across the entire event. Use them wisely!

---

## 4. Academic Integrity & Anti-Cheat Rules

To ensure a fair competition for all participants, all workstations run within the **ByteVerse Anti-Cheat Arena**:
- **Stay in Fullscreen:** Exiting fullscreen mode or switching tabs will immediately **lock your workstation**.
- **No External Aids:** The use of personal mobile devices, external browser tabs, notes, or search engines is strictly forbidden.
- **Station Unlocks:** If your station is locked due to an accidental tab switch or shortcut, raise your hand for an on-site Proctor to evaluate and unlock your station. Repeated violations will result in immediate disqualification.

---

## 5. Supported Programming Languages

For all coding rounds, you may submit solutions in any of the following languages:
- **C** (GCC 9.2.0)
- **C++** (GCC 9.2.0)
- **Java** (OpenJDK 13)
- **Python** (Python 3.8.1)

Good luck, coders! May the best duo win!
